SERVICES AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY RECORDS OFFICE AND DATAWORKS PVT LTD

1. The Vendor shall not disclose any citizen records or internal files of the Records Office to any outside person without written approval.

2. The Records Office shall pay each approved invoice within forty-five days of receiving it.

3. The Records Office may end this Agreement by giving sixty days' notice in writing if the Vendor fails to meet the agreed service levels.

4. The Vendor shall indemnify the Records Office against all claims and costs arising from errors made by its staff while digitising documents.

5. The Vendor's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid in the previous year.

6. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the courts at Bengaluru shall have jurisdiction.
